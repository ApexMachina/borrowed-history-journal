The three sources

Anything you know got in somehow. There are only three ways in.

Deduction

What you can work out yourself, step by step, from what you already hold. You can check every step. It only moves what you bring to it.

Any number times zero is zero.

Direct experience / intuition

What you saw, felt, or lived, here and now. Hard to doubt from the inside. It only shows the moment you were in.

My hand is cold.

Borrowed history

Everything someone handed you. Every book, teacher, map, date, headline, video and answer from a machine. You weren’t there. You took it on trust.

Australia exists.

How the journal works

Write one claim. Choose the source that is most honest. Look at the ratio. That is all it does.

If a claim seems to have two sources, ask which one actually carried it to you.

Your entries never leave this device. There are no accounts and no tracking. Export a copy whenever you like, and restore it on another device from the Review page.
